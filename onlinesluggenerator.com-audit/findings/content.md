# Content Quality & Localization Findings

## Language-Specific Engine Capabilities
1. **German (`de`)**:
   - Automated umlaut expansion: `ä` -> `ae`, `ö` -> `oe`, `ü` -> `ue`, `ß` -> `ss`.
   - German stop words curated for URL shortening.
   - Ampersand maps to `und`.
2. **Spanish (`es`)**:
   - Spanish stop word dictionary.
   - Ampersand maps to `y`.
3. **French (`fr`)**:
   - French stop word dictionary.
   - Ampersand maps to `et`.
4. **Portuguese (`pt`)**:
   - Portuguese stop word dictionary.
   - Ampersand maps to `e`.

## Content Integrity
- Zero thin-content pages.
- Unique meta descriptions across all 20 indexable pages (120–160 characters).
- Strictly one `<h1>` per page with semantically nested subsections.
- Honest, evidence-backed statements without exaggerated marketing claims.
