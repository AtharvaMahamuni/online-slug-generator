Facts a summary can quote

Presets reproduce WordPress, Django, python-slugify and npm slugify output on a published test set: https://github.com/AtharvaMahamuni/online-slug-generator/blob/main/tests/slug/fixtures/parity.json. The python-slugify preset matches python-slugify 9.2.0 on ASCII, Latin accents that fold, and punctuation from that test set; it shows “not matched” when it cannot. For non-Latin scripts, use python-slugify directly.
