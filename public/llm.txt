# Online Slug Generator

> Free browser-only URL slug tool. Google has said llms.txt does not help or hurt Google Search.

- [llms.txt](https://onlinesluggenerator.com/llms.txt): Pages and facts.
- [llms-full.txt](https://onlinesluggenerator.com/llms-full.txt): The same pages, with a short summary of each.
- [ai.txt](https://onlinesluggenerator.com/ai.txt): Crawler policy. robots.txt is authoritative.
- [identity.json](https://onlinesluggenerator.com/identity.json): Organization and Person. No street address.
- [brand.txt](https://onlinesluggenerator.com/brand.txt): Name and author accounts.
